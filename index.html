Definite articles: el, la, los, las

These mean the. They agree with the noun in gender and number.

SingularPlural
Masculineel doctorlos doctores
Femininela profesoralas profesoras
Making nouns plural
  • Vowel + s: profesora → profesoras
  • Consonant + es: doctor → doctores
  • Change the article too: el doctor → los doctores

Culture & Context

Saludos en el mundo hispano

A greeting can change depending on the situation. In a formal situation, some people may use un apretón de manos. Among friends or family, a greeting may also include un abrazo or un beso.

Do not assume every Spanish-speaking community greets in exactly the same way. Context and relationship matter.

Country-language connections used in class

  • Colombia → español
  • Brasil → portugués
  • Portugal → portugués
  • Francia → francés
  • China → chino
  • Japón → japonés
